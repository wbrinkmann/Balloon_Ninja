Project Proposal

Overview of the project.

What is the main idea of the project?

  • Fruit Ninja inspired - Throwing balloon into air and having a robot arm pop it (with needle or knife?)
  • We want to utilize machine vision to detect a balloon, mathematically predict its trajectory, and create an algorithm to have the robot arm move to the predicted position and pop the balloon. The stretch/complete idea is to have something very similar to fruit ninja where the arm is able to pop multiple balloons viewed by the camera.

Provide some motivation for your project. Why does your team want to pursue this? What possible applications does the project have?

  • We all need some whimsy in our lives and drawing back on a childhood favorite game was a fun concept idea. We all united under this initial idea and then as we explored the feasibility and concepts needed we found a large interest in the overlap. As all of us wish to pursue working with a physical robot, in specific the robotic arm, this project idea both satisfied a whimsical fun application and our learning objectives (outlined below).
  • This project is not directly applicable to the real world, however, the concepts which will be learned and the robotics arm manipulation are translatable to real world applications. This robot looks at finding a moving object, similarly in an orchard an apple may be swaying and a robot needs to pick it. Here, we are not picking, but the tracking and moving of the robot arm is similar. This can also be said for a manufacturing floor where as an item moves across the floor, a robot may need to identify it and pick it, similar to this project's intentions.

What topics will you explore, and what will you generate? What frameworks / algorithms are you planning to explore?

  • We will be exploring inverse kinematics to find what angle to move the arm to be able to pop the balloon
    • We will look into reinforcement learning to get the arm to move in an optimal (as fast as possible) path so that it can pop the most balloons.
  • Object detection - potentially combining color and shape detection (OpenCV functions)
  • Trajectory mapping - Kalman filter?
  • ROS2
  • Reinforcement learning

What is your MVP? What are your stretch goals?

  • The MVP will include a balloon being released from above the robot, it floating down, and the robot making a mark with a sharpie on it as it descends. Marker should poke it out of the “plane” that the balloons are moving in.
  • Stretch goals (ranked in order of implementation preference):
    • Using a needle to pop the balloon (for safety purposes everyone in the room will be required to wear safety glasses)
    • Balloons being thrown up (like in fruit ninja fruits are thrown up and then fall down)
    • Identify multiple balloons, different colors can be different points.
    • Being able to identify “bombs” or items thrown in that shouldn’t be popped. This may be achieved by using different balloon colors and only ‘targeting’ certain colors, or by including a stress ball or ballpit ball as distractors.
    • Using a knife to pop the balloon, like a slash. (yes, we promise to first write a safety protocol)
